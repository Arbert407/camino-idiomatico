# Native Phrases for General Use

This guide contains 100 common native English phrases for everyday conversation. Each phrase includes a definition, correct examples, and incorrect examples.

---

### 1. "Come along"

**Definition:** To accompany someone or go with them.

**Correct Examples:**
- Come along with me.
- She came along to the party.
- You should come along.
- He's coming along tomorrow.

**Incorrect Examples:**
- You should accompany me. ❌
- She accompanied to the party. ❌
- You should go with. ❌
- He's going with tomorrow. ❌
