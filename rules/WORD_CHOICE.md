# Word Choice for Natural English

This guide contains practical rules for making your English sound more natural through word choice and filler words. These are NOT grammar rules—they are functional usage guidelines that native speakers follow intuitively.

## Rules 26-99: Word Choice and Natural Expressions

### 26. Use "kind of" instead of "somewhat"

**Correct Examples:**
- It's kind of confusing.
- That's kind of expensive.
- It's kind of a big deal.
- He's kind of lazy.
- I kind of figured that out.

**Incorrect Examples:**
- It's somewhat confusing. ❌
- That's rather expensive. ❌
- It's to some degree a big deal. ❌
- He is somewhat lazy. ❌
- I somewhat figured that out. ❌

---

**Correct Examples:**

**Incorrect Examples:**

- He somewhat agreed. ❌

### 31. Use "I mean" to clarify

- I am saying that it is possible. ❌
- What else can we do, I ask? ❌
- It makes sense, I am saying. ❌
