# Native Phrases for IT and STEM

This guide contains 100 common native phrases used in IT and STEM contexts. Each phrase includes correct and incorrect usage examples.

---

### 1. "It works"

**Definition:** Used to indicate that something functions correctly or as expected.

**Correct Examples:**
- The patch works now.
- It works on my machine.
- The solution works perfectly.
- Does it work for you?

**Incorrect Examples:**
- The patch functions now. ❌
- It operates on my machine. ❌
- The solution functions perfectly. ❌
- Does it function for you? ❌

---
