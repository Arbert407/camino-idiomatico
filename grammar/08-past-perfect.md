# Past Perfect (I had done) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Past Perfect?

The Past Perfect is used to describe:
- **Actions completed before another action** in the past
- **The earlier past** of two past events

> "I **had finished** before the server crashed."
> "The system **had crashed** before we noticed."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + had + past participle

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I, You, We, They, He, She, It | had |

> ✅ I **had finished** → He **had completed**

---

## Negative

### Subject + had + not + past participle

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **had not (hadn't)** finished |
| They | They **hadn't** finished |

> ❌ "I didn't finished"
> ✅ "I **hadn't finished**"

---

## Questions

### Had + Subject + Past Participle?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| **Had** you finished? | Yes, I **had** / No, I **hadn't** |
| **What** had happened? | The system had crashed |

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Sequence of past events

> "I **had backed up** the data before the system failed."
> "We **had tested** everything before deployment."

### 2. Cause and effect in the past

> "The server crashed because we **had not updated** the software."
> "The bug remained because we **hadn't identified** the root cause."

### 3. Reported speech

> "She said she **had completed** the coding."
> "They told me they **had finished** testing."

### 4. Incident reports

> "By the time we realized, the virus **had already infected** several files."
> "The server **had shown** warning signs before the crash."

---

## Time Expressions with Past Perfect

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **before** | I had finished before she arrived |
| **by the time** | By the time we noticed, it had spread |
| **after** | After it had crashed, we analyzed |

---

## Past Simple vs Past Perfect

| Past Simple | Past Perfect |
|------------|------------|
| Single past event | Earlier past event |
| "I fixed the bug" | "I had fixed the bug before the meeting" |
| "The server crashed" | "The server had crashed before we arrived" |

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. I ___ (complete) the backup before the crash. → **had completed**
2. By the time we ___ (arrive), the server ___ (already/crash). → **arrived, had already crashed**
3. She said she ___ (finish) the project. → **had finished**
4. They ___ (not/save) the data before the failure. → **hadn't saved**
5. The system ___ (show) warnings before it failed. → **had shown**

## Key Differences Spanish → English

| Spanish | English |
|---------|--------|
| Yo había terminado | I **had finished** |
| Él ya había hecho | He **had already done** |
| ¿Habías terminado? | **Had** you **finished**? |
| No había terminado | I **hadn't finished** |

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using Past Perfect for single past event

> ❌ "I had worked yesterday" (when only one event)
> ✅ "I worked yesterday"

### ❌ Mistake: Confusing with Present Perfect

> ❌ "I have finished before" (use Past Perfect)
> ✅ "I had finished before the meeting"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong form

> ❌ "I had did the work"
> ✅ "I **had done** the work"
