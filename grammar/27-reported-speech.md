# Reported Speech: He Said That... - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Reported speech relays information without quoting directly. Essential for passing on messages, instructions, and updates.

---

## He said that...

### Structure

**Subject + said that + clause**

> ✅ "He **said that** the server would be down for maintenance."
> ✅ "She **said that** we need to update our passwords."

### Tense Changes (Backshift)

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "I am" | He said that he **was** |
| "I will" | He said that he **would** |
| "I have" | He said that he **had** |
| "I did" | He said that he **had** |

---

## Uses in IT

### Relaying Instructions
> "He **said that** we need to backup the data before the update."
> "She **said that** all employees must complete the training."

### Sharing Updates
> "He **said that** the new software will be launched next month."
> "They **said that** the security budget has been increased."

### Reporting Meetings
> "In yesterday's meeting, he **said that** network security needs improvement."
> "She **said that** the deadline has been extended."

---

## Key Points

1. **That** is optional: "He said (that) the server is down."
2. **Tense shift** typically happens in reporting
3. **Pronoun changes** may be needed

---

## Practice Exercises

### Report what was said:

1. "The server is down." → He **said that** the server **was down**.
2. "We need to update the software." → He **said that** we **needed to update** the software.
3. "The project will be delayed." → He **said that** the project **would be delayed**.
4. "I finished the testing." → She **said that** she **had finished** the testing.
5. "The team will handle the migration." → He **said that** the team **would handle** the migration.

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: No tense change when required
> ❌ "He said that the server is down" (if statement was in past)
> ✅ "He said that the server **was** down"

### ❌ Mistake: Using quotes
> ❌ "He said "the server is down"." (no quotes in reported speech)
> ✅ "He said that the server was down"

## Quick Summary

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|--------------|---------------|
| am | was |
| will | would |
| have | had |
| did | had |
| is going to | was going to |
