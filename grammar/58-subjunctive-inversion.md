# Subjunctive Mood and Inversion in IT - Grammar Guide

## Subjunctive Mood

### Uses: Wishes, Suggestions, Hypotheticals

**Structure:** Base verb (without "to") after expressions

### Expressions with Subjunctive

| Expression | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| **suggest** | "I suggest that he **review** the code." |
| **insist** | "It is essential that every member **understand**." |
| **recommend** | "I recommend that she **back up** data." |
| **demand** | "We demand that the team **be** trained." |
| **require** | "It's crucial that the data **be** secured." |

---

## Subjunctive Examples in IT

> "It is essential that every developer **test** the code."
> "I recommend that she **back up** the database."
> "It's vital that he **be** aware of security protocols."
> "We insist that the system **be** updated."

---

## Inversion

### Uses: Emphasis, Formal Style

**Structure:** Auxiliary + Subject verb

### Inversion Examples

| Normal | Inversion |
|--------|----------|
| "We need to upgrade the system" | "We do need to upgrade the system." |
| "Only after testing did we deploy." | "Only after testing, we deployed." |

### Negative Inversion

> "**Not only** does the software improve speed, but it also saves resources."
> "**Never** have we seen such a critical bug."
> "**Rarely** does the system fail."

### Conditional Inversion

> "**Had** we tested more, we **would have** found the bug."
> "**Were** I to choose, I'd pick cloud storage."

---

## Uses in IT

### Subjunctive
> "I recommend that he **review** the security protocols."
> "It's essential that every user **follow** the policy."

### Inversion
> "**Not only** does this application save time, but it also improves efficiency."
> "**Only** after extensive testing did we deploy to production."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete:

1. I suggest that she ___ the code. (review) → **review**
2. Not only does it save time, ___ it also improves efficiency. (and/but) → **but** or "it"
3. ___ we upgrade, we need more resources. (if/were) → **Were** (inversion)
4. It's essential that the data ___. (backup/be backed) → **be backed**

---

## Quick Summary

| Form | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Subjunctive** | Base verb after suggest/insist/recommend |
| **Inversion** | Auxiliary before subject for emphasis |
