# Past Continuous (I was doing) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Past Continuous?

The Past Continuous is used to describe:
- **Actions in progress** at a specific time in the past
- **Parallel actions** (two actions happening at the same time)
- **Background actions** interrupted by another action

> "I **was coding** when the server crashed."
> "The system **was running** when the error occurred."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + was/were + verb-ing

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I, He, She, It | was |
| We, You, They | were |

**Rule**: Add **-ing** to the verb

> ✅ I **was working** → We **were working**
> ✅ He **was testing** → They **were testing**

---

## Negative

### Subject + was/were + not + verb-ing

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **was not (wasn't)** working |
| You | You **were not (weren't)** working |
| He | He **wasn't** working |

> ❌ "I don't was working"
> ✅ "I **wasn't working**"

---

## Questions

### Wh-word + was/were + Subject + verb-ing?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|------------|
| **Were** you working? | Yes, I **was** / No, I **wasn't** |
| **What** were you doing? | I was testing |
| **When** was it happening? | It was happening at 3 PM |

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Past Continuous vs Past Simple?

Use Past Continuous for actions IN PROGRESS:
> ✅ "I **was coding** when it crashed" (coding was happening)

Use Past Simple for completed actions:
> ✅ "I **coded** all night" (completed work)

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Troubleshooting descriptions

> "I **was updating** the software when the error appeared."
> "The server **was running** slowly when it crashed."

### 2. Project status updates

> "I **was working** on the database last night."
> "We **were testing** the new module yesterday."

### 3. Incident reporting

> "The system **was showing** warning signs before the crash."
> "I **was monitoring** the server when the issue occurred."

### 4. Setting scene for past events

> "While I **was debugging**, my colleague was testing."
> "The server **was being** slow, so we restarted it."

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using state verbs in continuous

> ❌ "I was knowing the answer"
> ✅ "I **knew** the answer"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong was/were

> ❌ "He was working" for plural
> ✅ "They **were** working"

### ❌ Mistake: Forgetting -ing

> ❌ "He was work when I arrived"
> ✅ "He **was working** when I arrived"
