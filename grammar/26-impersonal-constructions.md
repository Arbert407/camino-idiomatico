# Impersonal Constructions in IT - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Impersonal constructions are used to discuss actions or responsibilities without directly naming the doer.

---

## It is said that...

### Use: General Beliefs/Reports

> "It **is said that** the new software can reduce processing time."
> "It **is said that** quantum computing will revolutionize encryption."

### Structure

**It is said that + clause**

> ✅ "It **is said that** our company is investing in blockchain."

---

## He is said to...

### Use: Describing Someone's Reputation

> "He **is said to be** an expert in cybersecurity."
> "He **is said to have** extensive database experience."

### Structure

**Subject + is said to + verb**

> ✅ "He **is said to be** very knowledgeable."

---

## He is supposed to...

### Use: Expected Actions/Duties

> "He **is supposed to** update the network protocols."
> "He **is supposed to** deliver the project next week."

### Structure

**Subject + is supposed to + verb**

> ✅ "She **is supposed to** lead the meeting."

---

## Have something done

### Use: Outsourcing/Delegation

> "We **have the server maintained** by a specialist."
> "We **have the security audited** annually."

### Structure

**Have + object + done (by someone)**

> ✅ "We **have the system checked** regularly."

---

## Summary

| Construction | Use | Example |
|--------------|-----|---------|
| **It is said that** | General beliefs | "It is said that..." |
| **He is said to** | Reputation | "He is said to be..." |
| **He is supposed to** | Expectations | "He is supposed to..." |
| **Have something done** | Outsourcing | "We have it done by..." |

---

## Uses in IT

### General Reports
> "It **is said that** the new update will improve performance."
> "It **is said that** the system is very efficient."

### Team Capabilities
> "John **is said to be** the best developer."
> "She **is said to have** extensive cloud experience."

### Responsibilities
> "He **is supposed to** handle the migration."
> "The team lead **is supposed to** present the plan."

### Outsourcing
> "We **have the security audited** by a third party."
> "We **have the server maintained** by an external vendor."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. It ___ said that the new system will improve speed. → **is**
2. He ___ said to be an expert in AI. → **is**
3. She ___ supposed to lead the project. → **is**
4. We have the server ___ by a specialist. → **maintained**
5. It ___ said that our team is the best. → **is**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong form
> ❌ "He is supposed do it" (wrong)
> ✅ "He **is supposed to do** it"

### ❌ Mistake: Missing "that"
> ✅ "It is said that..." (not "It is said...")
