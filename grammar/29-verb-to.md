# Verb + to and Verb (Object) + to - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

These structures express purpose, intention, and giving instructions to others.

---

## Verb + to

### Use: Expressing Intentions

> "We **plan to upgrade** the system."
> "I **need to update** the software."

### Structure

**Verb + to + verb**

> ✅ "We **aim to implement** new protocols."

---

## Verb (Object) + to

### Use: Directing Others

> "I **advised him to check** the network."
> "I **told the team to prepare** a report."

### Structure

**Verb + object + to + verb**

> ✅ "I **asked John to lead** the project."

---

## Key Differences

| Verb + to | Verb (Object) + to |
|----------|-------------------|
| Purpose/intention | Instructing others |
| "We need to do it" | "I told him to do it" |
| "I want to learn" | "I want you to learn" |

---

## Uses in IT

### Planning
> "We **plan to launch** the new app by the end of the quarter."
> "We **aim to complete** the migration next week."

### Delegating
> "I **assigned him to update** the client database."
> "I **asked the team to review** the code."

### Instructing
> "I **told everyone to backup** their data."
> "I **advised the client to consider** cloud storage."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. We need ___ (upgrade) the system. → **to upgrade**
2. I told him ___ (check) the logs. → **to check**
3. They plan ___ (implement) new security. → **to implement**
4. She advised me ___ (review) the code. → **to review**
5. We want ___ (complete) the project. → **to complete**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Missing "to"
> ❌ "I told him check the system" (wrong)
> ✅ "I **told him to check** the system"

### ❌ Mistake: Confusing forms
> "I want to do it" (I have the intention)
> "I want you to do it" (I'm asking someone else)

---

## Quick Summary

| Structure | Use | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| **Verb + to** | Intention | "We plan to do it" |
| **Verb + object + to** | Instruction | "I told him to do it" |
