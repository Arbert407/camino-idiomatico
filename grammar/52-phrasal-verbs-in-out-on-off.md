# Phrasal Verbs with In, Out, On, Off - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Phrasal verbs with these particles are common in IT operations.

---

## In/Out Phrasal Verbs

| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|------------|
| **log in** | access | "Log in to the system." |
| **log out** | exit | "Log out when done." |
| **plug in** | connect | "Plug in the device." |
| **unplug** | disconnect | "Unplug the printer." |
| **fill in** | complete | "Fill in the form." |
| **fill out** | complete | "Fill out the details." |

---

## On/Off Phrasal Verbs

| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|------------|
| **turn on** | start | "Turn on the server." |
| **turn off** | stop | "Turn off the device." |
| **switch on** | enable | "Switch on the firewall." |
| **switch off** | disable | "Switch off the network." |
| **log on** | access | "Log on to the network." |

---

## Uses in IT

### In/Out
> "Did you **log in** to the new system?"
> "Please **fill out** the configuration form."
> "**Unplug** the old printer."

### On/Off
> "**Turn on** the server for maintenance."
> "**Switch off** the wireless connection."
> "Don't forget to **log off** when leaving."

## Practice Exercises

### Choose the correct verb:

1. ___ in to the database. (log/fill) → **log**
2. ___ out the form. (fill/turn) → **fill**
3. ___ on the computer. (turn/run) → **turn**
4. ___ off the network. (switch/back) → **switch**
5. ___ the device. (plug/turn) → **plug** or **unplug**

| Type | Meaning |
