# IT Idiomatic Expressions - Complete Guide

Everyday expressions, slang, and sayings used in the tech industry.

**Level Key**: A2 = Basic, B1 = Intermediate, B2 = Advanced, C1 = Fluent

---

## A

### 1. "bandaid solution"
**Level**: B1
A temporary fix that doesn't address the root cause.

**Examples**:
> "That's just a **bandaid solution** - we need to fix the architecture."
> "Applying a bandaid to a bullet wound won't help."
> "This patch is just a bandaid, not a real fix."

---

### 2. "bleeding edge"
**Level**: B2
Even newer than cutting edge - taking risks with latest technology.

**Examples**:
> "We're working with **bleeding edge** AI technology."
> "Running bleeding edge software can be risky."
> "Only use bleeding edge in development."

---

### 3. "boil the ocean"
**Level**: B2
Trying to do something impossible or too ambitious.

**Examples**:
> "We can't **boil the ocean** - let's focus on the MVP."
> "Trying to please everyone is boiling the ocean."
> "That's too ambitious - you're trying to boil the ocean."

---

### 4. "break things"
**Level**: B1
To intentionally cause errors to test system resilience.

**Examples**:
> "We need to **break things** to ensure the system is robust."
> "DevOps culture encourages breaking things and learning."
> "Breaking things in staging is better than in production."

---

### 5. "building in public"
**Level**: B2
Sharing work progress openly during development.

**Examples**:
> "We're **building in public** - check our GitHub for updates."
> "Building in public helps get early feedback."
> "Start building in public to attract contributors."
