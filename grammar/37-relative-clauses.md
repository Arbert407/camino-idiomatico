# Relative Clauses with and without Who/That/Which - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Relative clauses provide additional information about nouns.

---

## With Who/That/Which (Defining Clauses)

### Use: Essential Information

> "The programmer **who fixed** the bug is skilled."
> "The server **that crashed** is now operational."

### Structure

**Noun + who/that/which + verb**

> ✅ "The software **that we installed** is working."  
> ✅ "The developer **who joined** us is skilled."

---

## Without Who/That/Which (Non-Defining)

### Use: Additional Information

> "Our latest project, **developed using** new technology, is successful."
> "The new intern, **coming from** a programming background, is quick to learn."

### Structure

**Noun, + verb-ing/ed + ,**

> ✅ "The report, **completed yesterday**, is ready."

---

## Key Differences

| With Who/That/Which | Without |
|--------------------|---------|
| Identifies/specifies | Adds extra info |
| Essential meaning | Optional |
| No commas | Commas used |

---

## Uses in IT

### With Who/That/Which
> "The server **that we upgraded** is running well."
> "The developer **who has experience** in AI is available."
> "The software **which we tested** is reliable."

### Without
> "Our database, **backed up regularly**, is secure."
> "The meeting, **held yesterday**, was productive."
> "The new feature, **released last week**, is popular."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. The server ___ crashed needs repair. → **that** (who/that)
2. Our software, ___ recently, is stable. → **updated** (without)
3. The developer ___ has experience is available. → **who**
4. The project, ___ last month, was successful. → **completed** (without)
5. The server ___ we upgraded is working. → **that**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Missing comma for non-defining
> "The software which we tested is ready" (defining essential)
> "The software, which we tested yesterday, is ready" (adds info)

### ❌ "who" for things
> "The software that crashed" (not "who crashed")

---

## Quick Summary

| Type | Structure | Commas |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Defining** | who/that/which | No |
| **Non-defining** | -ing/-ed | Yes |
