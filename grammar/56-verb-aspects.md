# The 4 Verb Aspects in IT - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

The four aspects show different time views in IT communication.

---

## 1. Simple Aspect

### Facts, Habits, General Truth

> "I **code** in Python."
> "The server **crashes** often."
> "She **writes** documentation."

---

## 2. Continuous Aspect

### Ongoing Actions, Temporary States

> "I **am coding** right now."
> "The server **is crashing** repeatedly."
> "She **is writing** the report."

---

## 3. Perfect Aspect

### Completed Actions with Present Relevance

> "I **have completed** the project."
> "The server **has crashed** twice."
> "She **has written** all docs."

---

## 4. Perfect Continuous

### Duration + Ongoing/Recent

> "I **have been working** on this for hours."
> "The server **has been crashing** since morning."
> "She **has been writing** all day."

---

## Summary Table

| Aspect | Form | Use | Example |
|--------|------|-----|---------|
| Simple | base/-s | facts, habits | "I code" |
| Continuous | am/is/are + verb-ing | ongoing | "I'm coding" |
| Perfect | have/has + past participle | completed | "I've coded" |
| Perfect Continuous | have been + verb-ing | duration | "I've been coding" |

---

## Uses in IT

### Simple
> "I **handle** database systems."

### Continuous
> "I **am working** on a network project."

### Perfect
> "I **have completed** the analysis."

### Perfect Continuous
> "I **have been analyzing** the data since last week."

## Practice Exercises

### Choose the correct aspect:

1. She ___ in Java. (code - simple present) → **codes**
2. I ___ the project for 3 hours. (work - perfect continuous) → **have been working**
3. The system ___. (crash - present perfect) → **has crashed**
4. We ___ the software. (test - present continuous) → **are testing**

---

## Quick Reference

| Aspect | Key Word | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Simple | always, usually, every | "I test daily" |
| Continuous | now, currently | "I'm testing now" |
| Perfect | already, yet, just | "I've tested it" |
| Perfect Continuous | for, since | "I've been testing" |
