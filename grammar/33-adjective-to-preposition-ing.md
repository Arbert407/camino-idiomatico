# Adjective + to and Preposition + -ing: Afraid to Do / Afraid of Doing - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

The choice between these forms changes the focus of fear.

---

## Afraid to Do

### Use: Fear of Performing Action

> "I am **afraid to update** the live server."
> "He is **afraid to take** responsibility."

### Structure

**Afraid to + verb** (action to be performed)

> ✅ "I'm afraid to **make** changes."

---

## Afraid of -ing

### Use: Fear of Process/Outcome

> "I am **afraid of causing** downtime."
> "She is **afraid of making** mistakes."

### Structure

**Afraid of + verb-ing** (the process)

> ✅ "I'm afraid of **causing** issues."

---

## Key Differences

| Afraid to Do | Afraid of -ing |
|--------------|---------------|
| Fear of action | Fear of process |
| "Afraid to do it" | "Afraid of doing it" |
| Hesitation | Concern about outcome |

---

## Uses in IT

### Afraid to Do
> "I'm **afraid to upgrade** during peak hours."
> "He's **afraid to sign off** on the project."
> "They're **afraid to adopt** the new software."

### Afraid of -ing
> "We're **afraid of disrupting** the network."
> "She's **afraid of losing** data."
> "I'm **afraid of overlooking** vulnerabilities."

## Practice Exercises

### Choose the correct form:

1. I am afraid ___ (make) changes. → **to make** (action)
2. I'm afraid ___ (cause) issues. → **of causing** (process)
3. She's afraid ___ (take) the lead. → **to take** (action)
4. They're afraid ___ (adjust) to new interface. → **of adjusting** (process)
5. He's afraid ___ (miss) the deadline. → **of missing** (process)

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Confusing forms
> "I'm afraid to do it" = I hesitate to do it
> "I'm afraid of doing it" = I'm concerned about the result

### ❌ Wrong: "I'm afraid to cause" (wrong)
> Correct: "I'm afraid **of causing**"

---

## Quick Summary

| Form | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **afraid to do** | The action itself |
| **afraid of doing** | The process or outcome |
