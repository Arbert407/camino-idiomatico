# Reporting Verbs in IT Context - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Reporting verbs relay information, suggestions, and warnings in IT.

---

## Suggest / Recommend

### Use: Proposing Ideas

| Reporting Verb | Structure | Example |
|-----------------|------------|---------|
| **suggest** | that + subject + verb | "I suggest that he **upgrade** the server." |
| **recommend** | that + subject + verb | "I recommend that we **use** cloud storage." |

### Alternative Forms

| + Gerund | + Infinitive |
|----------|--------------|
| "I suggest **upgrading**." | "I suggest **to upgrade**." (rare) |
| "I recommend **using**." | "I recommend **to use**." (rare) |

---

## State / Claim

### Use: Asserting Facts

| Reporting Verb | Structure | Example |
|-----------------|------------|---------|
| **state** | that + clause | "The manager **states** that the project is on track." |
| **claim** | that + clause | "The user **claims** that the app crashes." |

---

## Advise / Warn

### Use: Recommendations and Warnings

| Reporting Verb | Structure | Example |
|-----------------|------------|---------|
| **advise** | object + to + verb | "I **advise** you **to update** regularly." |
| **warn** | object + to + verb | "I **warn** you **not to skip** testing." |

### With about

> "I **advise** you **about** security risks."
> "I **warn** you **about** the potential issues."

---

## Uses in IT

### Suggest/Recommend
> "The analyst **suggests implementing** a new database."
> "I **recommend using** PostgreSQL for reliability."

### State/Claim
> "The project coordinator **states** that the deadline is next month."
> "The client **claims** that the system is slow."

### Advise/Warn
> "The security expert **advises** regular password changes."
> "I **warn** you **not to ignore** the warnings."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete:

1. The analyst ___ upgrading the server. (suggest) → **suggests**
2. The manager ___ that the project is on schedule. (state) → **states**
3. I ___ you to backup regularly. (advise) → **advise**
4. The user ___ that the app doesn't work. (claim) → **claims**
5. We ___ against skipping tests. (warn) → **warn**

---

## Quick Summary

| Verb | Use | Structure |
|------|-----|-----------|
| **suggest** | propose | suggest + that clause / -ing |
| **recommend** | suggest | recommend + that clause / -ing |
| **state** | assert | state + that clause |
| **claim** | assert | claim + that clause |
| **advise** | recommend | advise + object + to verb |
| **warn** | caution | warn + object + not to verb |
