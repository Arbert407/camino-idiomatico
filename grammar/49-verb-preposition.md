# Verb + Preposition: To, At, About, For, Of, After - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Prepositions after verbs change their meaning. Essential for IT communication.

---

## Verb + To

### Purpose

| Verb + to | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **send to** | direction |
| **transfer to** | move to |
| **connect to** | link to |

> "We need to **send the report to** the client."
> "**Connect the printer to** the network."

---

## Verb + At

### Target

| Verb + at | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **look at** | examine |
| **arrive at** | reach |
| **laugh at** | mock |

> "**Look at** the logs for errors."
> "**Arrive at** a decision."

---

## Verb + About

### Topic

| Verb + about | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **talk about** | discuss |
| **ask about** | inquire |
| **complain about** | protest |

> "We had a meeting **about the** new software."
> "**Ask about** the deployment schedule."

---

## Verb + For

### Purpose/Duration

| Verb + for | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **wait for** | anticipate |
| **search for** | look for |
| **pay for** | purchase |

> "The tool is **for monitoring** network traffic."
> "**Wait for** approval."

---

## Verb + Of

### Belonging

| Verb + of | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **consist of** | comprise |
| **dream of** | imagine |
| **approve of** | permit |

> "The system **consists of** multiple modules."
> "The team **approved of** the plan."

---

## Verb + After

### Sequence

| Verb + after | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **look after** | care for |
| **run after** | pursue |
| **be after** | seek |

> "**After reviewing** the code, we found bugs."
> "**Look after** the server while I'm away."

---

## Uses in IT

### Look At / About / For / Of / After
> "**Look at** the error message."
> "**Talk about** the security patch."
> "**Wait for** the update."
> "The report **consists of** several sections."
> "**After testing**, we deployed."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete:

1. Send the report ___ the client. → **to**
2. Look ___ the logs. → **at**
3. Wait ___ approval. → **for**
4. The system consists ___ modules. → **of**
5. After ___ the code, we test. → **reviewing**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "consist from" (wrong)
> ✅ "consist **of**"

### ❌ "ask to him" (when asking about)
> ✅ "ask **about** it"

---

## Quick Summary

| Pattern | Use |
|---------|-----|
| verb + to | direction |
| verb + at | target |
| verb + about | topic |
| verb + for | purpose |
| verb + of | belonging |
| verb + after | sequence |
