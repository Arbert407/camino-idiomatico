# Prepositions of Time: At, On, In - Grammar Guide

## At

### Specific Time Points

> "The meeting is **at** 3 PM."
> "The system updates **at** midnight."
> "**At** the moment, we're testing."

### Common Uses

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| at + time | at 10 AM, at noon, at night |

---

## On

### Days and Dates

> "The conference is **on** Monday."
> "We launch **on** April 15th."
> "**On** the first day, we presented."

### Common Uses

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| on + day | on Monday, on Tuesday |
| on + date | on January 1st |
| on + day + time | on Monday at 3 PM |

---

## In

### Longer Periods

> "The project starts **in** April."
> "We completed it **in** six months."
> "**In** the morning, we test."

### Common Uses

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| in + month/year | in April, in 2024 |
| in + morning/afternoon/evening | in the morning |
| in + period | in two weeks, in six months |

---

## Phrases

### On Time vs In Time

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **on time** | punctually, at the scheduled time |
| **in time** | before a deadline, just in time |

> "The project was completed **on time**."
> "We finished **in time** for the deadline."

### At the End vs In the End

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **at the end** | conclusion of a period |
| **in the end** | finally, after all |

> "**At the end** of the meeting, we decided."
> "**In the end**, we chose the cloud solution."

---

## Uses in IT

### At
> "The server restarts **at** midnight."
> "**At** the start, we tested everything."
> "**At** present, we're evaluating options."

### On
> "The release is **on** Monday."
> "We deploy **on** the 15th."
> "**On** weekdays, we work remotely."

### In
> "The project completes **in** March."
> "**In** the meantime, test the system."
> "**In** the long term, this saves money."

## Practice Exercises

### Choose the correct preposition:

1. The meeting is ___ 3 PM. → **at**
2. We launch ___ Monday. → **on**
3. The project starts ___ April. → **in**
4. We finished ___ time for the deadline. → **in time**
5. ___ the end, we decided. → **At the end** or "In the end"

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "in the Monday" (wrong)
> ✅ "**on** Monday"

### ❌ "at this week" (wrong)
> ✅ "**in** this week"

### ❌ "at 2024" (wrong)
> ✅ "**in** 2024"

---

## Quick Summary

| Preposition | Use |
|------------|-----|
| **at** | specific times |
| **on** | days/dates |
| **in** | months/years/periods |
| **on time** | punctually |
| **in time** | before deadline |
| **at the end** | conclusion |
| **in the end** | finally |
