# Prepositions of Movement: To, From, Through, Across, Into, Out Of, Towards, Away From, Around - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

These prepositions describe the direction and flow of data, tasks, and processes.

---

## To / From

### Direction

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **to** | toward | "Move data **to** the server" |
| **from** | origin | "Data comes **from** the database" |

---

## Through

### Movement Inside/Passing

> "The virus spread **through** the network."
> "Data moves **through** the firewall."

---

## Across

### From One Side to Another

> "The cable runs **across** the room."
> "Connect **across** the network."

---

## Into / Out Of

### Movement In/Out

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **into** | entering | "Upload **into** the cloud" |
| **out of** | exiting | "Extract **out of** the database" |

---

## Towards / Away From

### Direction

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **towards** | in direction of |
| **away from** | opposite direction |

> "Resources are allocated **towards** the project."
> "We're moving **away from** traditional methods."

---

## Around

### Circular Movement

> "The robot moved **around** the data center."
> "The data goes **around** the network."

---

## Uses in IT

### To/From
> "We're transferring files **to** the cloud."
> "The request came **from** the client."

### Through
> "Data passes **through** the gateway."

### Into/Out Of
> "Import data **into** the system."
> "Export files **out of** the database."

### Towards/Away From
> "We are moving **towards** cloud computing."
> "Shifting **away from** legacy systems."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. Data flows ___ the network. → **through**
2. Move files ___ the cloud. → **to** or **into**
3. Extract data ___ the old system. → **out of**
4. We're moving ___ the new system. → **towards**
5. The cable runs ___ the room. → **across**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "to" vs "into"
> "Go to the room" (direction)
> "Go into the room" (movement inside)

### ❌ "through" vs "across"
> "through" = inside/passing through
> "across" = from one side to another (surface)
