# Verb + Preposition + -ing: Succeed in -ing, Insist on -ing - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

These structures describe actions in relation to specific verbs and prepositions.

---

## Succeed in -ing

### Use: Successful Completion

> "We **succeeded in implementing** the new software."
> "I **succeeded in completing** my certification."

### Structure

**Succeed in + verb-ing**

> ✅ "They **succeeded in deploying** the system on time."

---

## Insist on -ing

### Use: Firm Determination

> "She **insists on testing** the program thoroughly."
> "He **insists on maintaining** high security standards."

### Structure

**Insist on + verb-ing**

> ✅ "We **insist on following** strict protocols."

---

## Key Differences

| Succeed in -ing | Insist on -ing |
|----------------|---------------|
| Achievement | Determination |
| "We succeeded in..." | "We insist on..." |
| Result of effort | Firm stance |

---

## Uses in IT

### Succeed in -ing - Achievements
> "We **succeeded in reducing** system downtime."
> "They **succeeded in launching** the app without issues."
> "I **succeeded in optimizing** the database."

### Insist on -ing - Best Practices
> "Our team **insists on conducting** thorough testing."
> "Management **insists on encrypting** all data."
> "I **insist on reviewing** all code personally."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. We succeeded ___(implement) the new system. → **in implementing**
2. She insists ___(test) every feature thoroughly. → **on testing**
3. They succeeded ___(deploy) ahead of schedule. → **in deploying**
4. He insists ___(maintain) security standards. → **on maintaining**
5. We succeeded ___(complete) the project on time. → **in completing**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using infinitive
> ❌ "I succeed to implement" (wrong)
> ✅ "I **succeeded in implementing**"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong preposition
> ❌ "I insist to test" (wrong)
> ✅ "I **insist on testing**"
