# Reflexive Pronouns and Expressions of Ownership - Grammar Guide

## Reflexive Pronouns

| Subject | Pronoun |
|---------|--------|
| I | myself |
| you (singular) | yourself |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| they | themselves |
| you (plural) | yourselves |

---

## Use: Subject = Object

### Action by Subject

> "I fixed the bug **myself**." (no help)
> "She configured the server **herself**."
> "They handled it **themselves**."

### Structure

**Subject + reflexive pronoun**

> ✅ "He did it **himself**."

---

## Expressions of Ownership

### A Friend of Mine / Our / Theirs

> "**A friend of mine** works in cybersecurity."
> "It's **my own** project."
> "I work **on my own**." (independently)
> "I did it **by myself**." (alone, no help)

### My Own / On My Own / By Myself

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| my own | personal possession |
| on my own | independently |
| by myself | without help |

---

## Uses in IT

### Reflexive Pronouns
> "I configured the network **myself**."
> "The team trained **themselves** in the new software."
> "She handles troubleshooting **herself**."

### Ownership Expressions
> "A colleague **of mine** helped with the project."
> "I prefer to work on my **own** on sensitive tasks."
> "I completed it **by myself**."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. I fixed the issue ___. → **myself**
2. She configured the server ___. → **herself**
3. A colleague of ___ helped. → **mine**
4. We completed the project ___. → **ourselves** or "by ourselves"
5. He works on his ___. → **own**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "I did it by my self" (wrong)
> ✅ "I did it **myself**" or "by **myself**"

### Missing Reflexive
> "He hurt himself" (correct) vs "He hurt him" (incorrect for reflexive)
