# Present Perfect vs Past Simple - Grammar Guide

## The Key Difference

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|----------------|------------|
| Actions with **unspecified time** | Actions at a **specific time** |
| Past actions **relevant to now** | Completed past actions |
| Experience **up to now** | Clear **timeline** |

---

## When to Use Present Perfect

### 1. Recent actions with present relevance
> "I **have updated** the software." (now it's updated)
> "I **have fixed** the bug." (now it's fixed)

### 2. Life experiences (no specific time)
> "I **have worked** with Java."
> "I **have attended** many conferences."

### 3. Actions continuing to present
> "I **have worked** here for 5 years."
> "She **has been** learning since 2020.

### 4. With yet, already, just, ever, never
> "Have you **finished** yet?"
> "I **have already** deployed."
> "I **have never** seen this error."

---

## When to Use Past Simple

### 1. Specific time in the past
> "I **finished** the project **last week**."
> "The server **crashed** **yesterday**."

### 2. Sequential past actions
> "First, I **tested** the code, then I **deployed** it."
> "I **woke up**, I **checked** the logs, I **found** the bug."

### 3. Completed actions with clear timeline
> "I **completed** the training **two years ago**."
> "We **launched** the app **in 2023**."

---

## Time Expressions Guide

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|-----------------|------------|
| **for** three years | **yesterday**, **last week** |
| **since** 2020 | **in 2020** |
| **just**, **already**, **yet** | **then**, **after** |
| **ever**, **never** | **ago** |

---

## Uses in IT Context

### Present Perfect for Skills/Experience
> "I **have developed** several applications." (current skill)
> "I **have worked** with databases." (relevant experience)

### Past Simple for Specific Projects
> "I **developed** that app in 2022." (specific project)
> "I **worked** on that project last year." (specific past)

### Present Perfect for Ongoing Situations
> "I **have been** dealing with this issue." (still relevant)
> "The server **has been** running slow." (current state)

### Past Simple for Completed Events
> "The server **went down** yesterday."
> "I **fixed** the bug last night."

---

## Comparison: IT Scenarios

| Scenario | Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| Current skills | "I **have learned** Python." | - |
| Past project | - | "I **learned** Python in 2020." |
| Current issue | "I **have been working** on this." | - |
| Fixed issue | - | "I **worked** on it yesterday." |
| Experience | "I **have seen** this before." | - |
| Past event | - | "I **saw** it last week." |

## Practice Exercises

### Choose the correct tense:

1. I ___ (work) here for 5 years. → **have worked**
2. I ___ (complete) the project last month. → **completed**
3. ___ you ever ___ (use) this software? → **Have, used**
4. I ___ (fix) the bug yesterday. → **fixed**
5. I ___ (be) testing the system all morning. → **have been testing**

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Specific time with Present Perfect
> ❌ "I have finished yesterday"
> ✅ "I finished yesterday"

### ❌ Mistake: No specific time with Past Simple
> ❌ "I worked with Java before"
> ✅ "I have worked with Java before"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong time expression
> ❌ "I have worked since 5 years"
> ✅ "I have worked for 5 years"
