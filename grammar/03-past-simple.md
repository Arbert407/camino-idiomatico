# Past Simple (I did) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Past Simple?

The Past Simple is used to describe:
- **Completed actions** in the past
- **Specific times** when things happened
- **Past habits** or states that are no longer true

> "I **completed** the project last week."
> "The server **crashed** yesterday."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + Verb (past form)

| Subject | Verb Form |
|---------|-----------|
| I, You, We, They | worked |
| He, She, It | worked |

**Rule**: Add **-ed** to regular verbs

> ✅ I **worked** → He **worked**
> ✅ They **tested** → She **tested**

---

## Important Exceptions

### Irregular Verbs

| Base Form | Past Simple |
|----------|------------|
| write | wrote |
| make | made |
| take | took |
| come | came |
| go | went |
| have | had |
| be | was/were |

> ✅ He **wrote** the code.
> ✅ The server **went** down.

---

## Spelling Rules for -ed

| Rule | Example |
|------|--------|
| Base verb + **-ed** | worked, tested |
| Short verb + consonant doubling | stopped, pinned |
| Verbs ending in **-e** | used, made |

> ✅ I **stopped** the process.
> ✅ He **pinned** the issue.

---

## Negative

### Subject + did + not + base verb

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **did not (didn't)** work |
| You | You **didn't** work |
| He | He **didn't** work |

> ❌ "I don't worked"
> ✅ "I **didn't work**"

> ❌ "He doesn't worked"
> ✅ "He **didn't work**"

---

## Questions

### Did + Subject + Base Verb?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| **Did** you finish? | Yes, I **did** / No, I **didn't** |
| **Did** it work? | Yes, it **did** / No, it **didn't** |

> **Did** you fix the bug?
> **Did** the server crash?

---

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Why "did" instead of "didn't" in questions?

Because "did" already contains the past meaning:

> ❌ "Did you **worked** yesterday?"
> ✅ "Did you **work** yesterday?"

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Reporting completed tasks

> "I **completed** the migration last week."
> "She **finished** the coding yesterday."

### 2. Problem resolution

> "I **resolved** the network issue."
> "We **fixed** the bug."

### 3. Past project milestones

> "We **launched** the app in 2023."
> "I **joined** the company two years ago."

### 4. Technical reviews

> "The server **crashed** three times last month."
> "The system **showed** downtime."

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Adding -s with did

> ❌ "He **didn't works**"
> ✅ "He **didn't work**"

### ❌ Mistake: Using past continuous for past actions

> ❌ "I was working on the project yesterday" (when it's complete)
> ✅ "I **worked** on the project yesterday"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong irregular form

> ❌ "He ** goed** to the meeting"
> ✅ "He **went** to the meeting"
