# Present Perfect (I have done) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Present Perfect?

The Present Perfect is used to describe:
- **Actions at an unspecified time** in the past
- **Actions that started in the past** and continue to present
- **Life experiences** up to now

> "I **have completed** the report." (result matters now)
> "I **have worked** with Python before." (experience)

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + have/has + past participle

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I, You, We, They | have |
| He, She, It | has |

> ✅ I **have worked** → He **has worked**
> ✅ They **have finished** → She **has finished**

---

## Negative

### Subject + have/has + not + past participle

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **have not (haven't)** worked |
| You | You **haven't** worked |
| He | He **has not (hasn't)** worked |

> ❌ "I don't have worked"
> ✅ "I **haven't worked**"

> ❌ "He doesn't has worked"
> ✅ "He **hasn't worked**"

---

## Questions

### Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| **Have** you finished? | Yes, I **have** / No, I **haven't** |
| **Has** it worked? | Yes, it **has** / No, it **hasn't** |

---

## Important Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **just** | I have just finished |
| **already** | I have already started |
| **yet** | Have you finished yet? |
| **ever** | Have you ever used...? |
| **never** | I have never seen this |
| **for** | I have worked here for years |
| **since** | I have worked here since 2020 |

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Completed tasks with present relevance

> "I **have updated** the software." (now it's updated)
> "We **have completed** the migration."

### 2. Ongoing projects

> "I **have been working** on this project for months."
> "She **has been** learning Python."

### 3. Professional experience

> "I **have worked** with Java before."
> "I **have dealt with** similar issues."

### 4. Lifetime experiences

> "I **have attended** several conferences."
> "I **have built** many applications."

| Spanish | English |
|---------|--------|
| Yo he trabajado | I **have worked** |
| Él ha terminado | He **has finished** |
| ¿Has terminado? | **Have** you **finished**? |
| No he terminado | I **haven't finished** |

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Specific time with Present Perfect

> ❌ "I have finished yesterday"
> ✅ "I **finished** yesterday" (use Past Simple)

### ❌ Mistake: Using -s with has

> ❌ "He has works hard"
> ✅ "He **has worked** hard"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong past participle

> ❌ "I have did the work"
> ✅ "I **have done** the work"
