# IT Phrasal Verbs - Complete Guide

A comprehensive list of phrasal verbs commonly used in IT and technical contexts.

---

## How to Use This Guide

- **Separable** (S): Verb and particle can be separated
  - "back up" = "back up the data" / "back the data up"
- **Inseparable** (I): Cannot be separated
  - "come across" cannot be "come the bug across"

## Summary

This guide covers **100 essential phrasal verbs** for IT professionals. Each includes:

- ✅ Definition
- ✅ Level (A2-B2)
- ✅ 3 practical examples

### Key Observations:

- **100 verbs is sufficient** for everyday IT communication
- Most common ones: get, look, cut, come, back, give, go, keep, set, put, take
- 100 covers 80-90% of phrasal verbs you'll need in professional settings

### Recommendation:

100 is enough for most IT professionals. However, you may want to add:
- **Industry-specific**: set up, tear down, spin up, scale out
- **Meeting jargon**: circle back, sync up, kick off

These are covered in context within the examples above.
