# Present Perfect Continuous (I have been doing) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Present Perfect Continuous?

The Present Perfect Continuous is used to describe:
- **Actions that started in the past** and are still continuing
- **Actions that recently stopped** but have an effect on the present
- **Duration** of ongoing activities

> "I **have been working** on this for 3 hours."
> "The server **has been running** slow."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + have been + verb-ing

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I, You, We, They | have been |
| He, She, It | has been |

> ✅ I **have been working** → He **has been working**

---

## Negative

### Subject + have/has + not + been + verb-ing

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **haven't been** working |
| He | He **hasn't been** working |

> ❌ "I am not been working"
> ✅ "I **haven't been working**"

---

## Questions

### Have/Has + Subject + been + verb-ing?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| **Have** you been working? | Yes, I **have** / No, I **haven't** |
| **How long** has it been running? | It has been running for hours |

---

## Time Expressions with Present Perfect Continuous

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **for** | for three hours |
| **since** | since morning |
| **all day/week** | all morning |

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Ongoing projects with duration

> "I **have been developing** this app for 6 months."
> "We **have been testing** since last week."

### 2. Recent activities with present effect

> "I **have been troubleshooting** all morning, that's why I'm tired."
> "I **have been setting up** the server."

### 3. Continuous problem-solving

> "I **have been dealing with** this issue for days."
> "The team **has been collaborating** closely."

### 4. Continuous learning

> "I **have been learning** Python this year."
> "She **has been studying** cloud computing."

---

## Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| I have worked (result) | I have been working (duration) |
| I have fixed the bug (complete) | I have been fixing the bug (ongoing) |
| She has written code (experience) | She has been writing code (activity) |

### Complete the sentences:

1. He ___ (work) on this project for months. → **has been working**

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using state verbs

> ❌ "I have been knowing the answer"
> ✅ "I **have known** the answer"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong form

> ❌ "I have being working"
> ✅ "I **have been working**"

### ❌ Mistake: Confusing with Present Perfect

> "I have worked" (result) vs "I have been working" (duration)
