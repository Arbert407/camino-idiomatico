# Pronouns and Determiners - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Pronouns replace nouns; determiners clarify what nouns refer to.

---

## Pronouns

### Personal Pronouns

| Subject | Object | Possessive |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| I | me | my |
| you | you | your |
| he | him | his |
| she | her | her |
| it | it | its |
| we | us | our |
| they | them | their |

### Demonstrative Pronouns

| This | That | These | Those |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| near | far | near (pl) | far (pl) |

> "**This** software is better."  
> "**Those** servers need upgrading."

---

## Determiners

### Possessive Determiners

> "**My** report is ready."  
> "**Your** feedback is appreciated."  
> "**Our** team is efficient."

### Other Determiners

| Determiner | Use |
|------------|-----|
| this/that | Specific items |
| these/those | Plural specific |
| some | Unspecified quantity |
| any | Question/negative |

> "Check **this** error log."
> "We need **some** resources."

---

## Uses in IT

### Pronouns
> "I tested the software, and **it** works fine."
> "I spoke with the client. **They** are happy."
> "The report? **It** is ready."

### Determiners
> "**My** analysis shows improvements."
> "**Their** system is down."
> "**This** server needs maintenance."

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. I emailed ___ the report. (you/your) → **you**
2. ___ analysis is complete. (my/your) → **My**
3. The server is down. Check ___ logs. (this/these) → **these**
4. I spoke with the client. ___ are happy. (they/their) → **They**
5. ___ feedback is appreciated. (your/you) → **Your**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "It's a good idea" vs "Its a good idea"
> "It's" = "It is"  
> "Its" = possessive

### ❌ Missing determiner
> **The** report, not "Report is ready"
