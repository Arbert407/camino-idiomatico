# Past Perfect Continuous (I had been doing) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Past Perfect Continuous?

The Past Perfect Continuous is used to describe:
- **Actions ongoing for a period** before another past action
- **Duration** of past activities
- **Cause** of a past situation

> "I **had been working** for hours when the server crashed."
> "We **had been testing** for weeks before the release."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + had been + verb-ing

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I, You, We, They, He, She, It | had been |

> ✅ I **had been working** → He **had been testing**

---

## Negative

### Subject + had + not + been + verb-ing

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **hadn't been** working |
| They | They **hadn't been** testing |

> ❌ "I was not been working"
> ✅ "I **hadn't been working**"

---

## Questions

### Had + Subject + been + verb-ing?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| **Had** you been working? | Yes, I **had** / No, I **hadn't** |
| **How long** had it been running? | It had been running for hours |

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Prolonged actions interrupted

> "I **had been working** on the project when it was cancelled."
> "We **had been developing** the app for two years."

### 2. Cause of past situations

> "The server crashed because we **had been receiving** unusually high traffic."
> "The system slowed down because we **had been running** too many processes."

### 3. Project timelines

> "We **had been progressing** well before the budget cuts."
> "I **had been investigating** for days before I found the cause."

### 4. Continuous effort

> "She **had been learning** Python for months before she got the certification."
> "They **had been collaborating** on the project since early 2023."

---

## Past Perfect vs Past Perfect Continuous

| Past Perfect | Past Perfect Continuous |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Completed action | Ongoing action |
| "I had finished" | "I had been finishing" |
| Result | Duration/Process |
| "The bug had been fixed" | "I had been fixing the bug for hours" |

---

## Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **for** | for several hours |
| **since** | since morning |
| **before** | before the crash |
| **by the time** | by the time we arrive |

## Practice Exercises

### Complete the sentences:

1. I ___ (work) on the issue for days before I solved it. → **had been working**
2. The server ___ (run) slow because it ___ (receive) high traffic. → **was running, had been receiving**
3. We ___ (test) for weeks before we ___ (find) the bug. → **had been testing, found**
4. ___ you ___ (troubleshoot) long before the fix? → **Had, been troubleshooting**
5. She ___ (not/use) the system before the error. → **hadn't been using**

## Key Differences Spanish → English

| Spanish | English |
|---------|--------|
| Yo había estado trabajando | I **had been working** |
| Él había estado debugueando | He **had been debugging** |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo habías estado...? | How long **had you been** working? |
| No había estado trabajando | I **hadn't been working** |

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using state verbs

> ❌ "I had been knowing the problem"
> ✅ "I **had known** the problem"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong form

> ❌ "I have being working"
> ✅ "I **had been working**"

### ❌ Mistake: For simple sequence, use Past Perfect

> ❌ "I had been finished before the meeting"
> ✅ "I **had finished** before the meeting"
