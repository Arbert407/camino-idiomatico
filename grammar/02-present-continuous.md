# Present Continuous (I am doing) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Present Continuous?

The Present Continuous is used to describe:
- **Actions happening right now**
- **Temporary situations**
- **Near-future plans**

> "I **am coding** right now."
> "The server **is running** slow."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

| Subject | Form |
|---------|------|
| I | am |
| He, She, It | is |
| We, You, They | are |

**Rule**: Add **-ing** to the verb

> ✅ I **am working** → He **is working**
> ✅ They **are testing**

---

## Important Exceptions

### Verbs ending in **-e**

Remove **e** and add **-ing**:

| Verb | Present Continuous |
|------|----------------|
| make | making |
| write | writing |
| use | using |

> ✅ She **is making** changes.
> ✅ He **is writing** code.

---

## Spelling Rules for -ing

| Rule | Example |
|------|--------|
| Base verb + **-ing** | working, testing |
| Short verb + consonant doubling | running, sitting |
| Verbs ending in **-e** | making, writing |

> ✅ I **am running** tests.
> ✅ He **is sitting** in the meeting.

---

## Negative

### Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **am not (I'm not)** working |
| You | You **are not (aren't)** working |
| He | He **is not (isn't)** working |

> ❌ I **am not** understand the problem
> ✅ I **am understanding** the problem

> ❌ He **doesn't working**
> ✅ He **is working**

---

## Questions

### Wh-word + am/is/are + Subject + verb-ing?

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|------------|
| **Are** you working? | Yes, I **am** / No, I'm not |
| **Is** it running? | Yes, it **is** / No, it isn't |
| **What** are you doing? | I'm testing the API |

---

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Why "understanding" not used in continuous with "understand"?

Because "understand" is a state verb, not an action verb:

> ❌ "I **am understanding** the problem"
> ✅ "I **understand** the problem"

## Uses in IT

### 1. Actions happening now

> "I **am coding** the new feature."
> "The server **is loading** slowly."

### 2. Temporary projects

> "I **am working** on a special project this month."
> "She **is handling** the database migration."

### 3. Planned future

> "I **am presenting** the strategy tomorrow."
> "We **are launching** the app next week."

### 4. At the moment of speaking

> "I **am debugging** the code right now."
> "The system **is processing** your request."

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: Using state verbs in continuous

> ❌ "I **am knowing** the answer"
> ✅ "I **know** the answer"

### ❌ Mistake: Permanent situations

> ❌ "I **am working** as a developer for 5 years"
> ✅ "I **work** as a developer"

### ❌ Mistake: Wrong verb form

> ❌ "He doesn't working"
> ✅ "He **isn't working**"
