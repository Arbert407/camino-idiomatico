# Present Simple (I do) - Grammar Guide

## What is the Present Simple?

The Present Simple is used to describe:
- **Habits** and routines
- **General facts** (truths)
- **Permanent states**

> "I **work** as a developer."
> "The server **crashes** often."

---

## Affirmative Structure

### Subject + Verb

| Subject | Verb Form |
|--------|-----------|
| I, You, We, They | work, run, make |
| He, She, It | works, runs, makes |

**Rule**: Add **-s** to the verb with **he/she/it**

> ✅ I **work** → He **works**
> ✅ They **use** → She **uses**

---

## Important Exceptions

### Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o

Add **-es** instead of -s:

| Verb | + he/she/it |
|-------|-----------|
| watch | watches |
| fix | fixes |
| go | goes |
| miss | misses |

> ✅ He **watches** the system.
> ✅ The server **fixes** the bug.

---

### Verbs ending in consonant + **y**

Change **y** to **i** + **-es**:

| Infinitive | + he/she/it |
|------------|--------------|
| study | studies |
| apply | applies |
| verify | verifies |

> ✅ She **studies** Java.
> ✅ He **applies** the patch.

---

## Negative

### Subject + do + not + verb

| Subject | Negative |
|---------|----------|
| I | I **do not (don't)** work |
| You | You **do not (don't)** work |
| He | He **does not (doesn't)** work |

> ❌ I **don't** understand the error.
> ❌ She **doesn't** know the password.

---

## Questions

### Do / Does + Subject + Verb?

| Question | Short Answer |
|---------|--------------|
| **Do** you know Python? | Yes, I **do** / No, I **don't** |
| **Does** it work? | Yes, it **does** / No, it **doesn't** |

> **Do** you have access?
> **Does** the API return data?

---

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Why "runs" not "run"?

Because with **he/she/it**, the verb always takes **-s**:

> ❌ Wrong: "He **run** the program"
> ✅ Correct: "He **runs** the program"

---

## Uses in IT

### 1. Describing your job

> "I **work** as a backend developer."
> "She **specializes** in DevOps."

### 2. Explaining functions

> "The function **returns** a value."
> "The API **processes** requests."

### 3. States and facts

> "The server **needs** more memory."
> "This tool **helps** with debugging."

### 4. Habits and routines

> "I **check** the logs daily."
> "He **runs** tests every morning."

### 5. Preferences

> "I **prefer** Linux over Windows."
> "She **likes** automated testing."

## Key Differences Spanish → English

| Spanish | English |
|---------|--------|
| Yo trabajo | I **work** |
| Él trabaja | He **works** (with -s) |
| Tú trabajas | You **work** (no -s) |
| ¿Trabajas? | **Do** you **work**? |
| No trabajo | I **don't** work |

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: forgetting -s in 3rd person

> ❌ "He **write** code every day"
> ✅ "He **writes** code every day"

### ❌ Mistake: Using doesn't without -s

> ❌ "He doesn't **works** properly"
> ✅ "He doesn't **work** properly"

### ❌ Mistake: Double negative

> ❌ "I don't know nothing"
> ✅ "I don't know anything"
