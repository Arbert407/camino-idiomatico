# Time-Related Expressions: As, As If, During, For, While, By, Until, By The Time - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

These expressions describe when and how events occur in time.

---

## As

### During Same Time

> "**As** I was reviewing the code, I found the bug."
> "The system crashed **as** I was updating."

**As if** = like, simulating

> "He acted **as if** he knew the answer."
> "It looks **as if** it's working."

---

## During

### Within a Period

> "**During** the meeting, we discussed the issue."
> "**During** our last update, we found problems."

---

## For

### Duration

> "We need to test **for** three hours."
> "The project will last **for** six months."

---

## While

### During Same Time (Action)

> "**While** updating the software, keep an eye on the server."
> "**While** I was working, the system crashed."

---

## By

### Deadline

> "Finish **by** Friday."
> "Complete the project **by** end of month."

---

## Until

### Up to Point

> "Work **until** it's fixed."
> "Wait **until** the system is ready."

---

## By the Time

### At Reference Point

> "**By the time** the meeting starts, I'll be ready."
> "**By the time** you read this, it will be done."

---

## Uses in IT

### As/During/While
> "**As** we were deploying, we monitored the system."
> "**During** the test, check for errors."
> "**While** working on the fix, back up data."

### By/Until
> "Complete it **by** tomorrow."
> "Continue testing **until** it passes."

### By the Time
> "**By the time** production begins, we'll be ready."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete:

1. We worked ___ the system was down. → **while**
2. Finish ___ Friday. → **by**
3. ___ the meeting, we discussed the issue. → **During**
4. We test ___ two hours. → **for**
5. ___ the time we launch, it'll be ready. → **By the time**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "while" vs "during"
> "During the meeting" (within time period)
> "While we were meeting" (simultaneous actions)

### ❌ "by" vs "until"
> "By Friday" = deadline (on or before Friday)
> "Until Friday" = up to Friday (up to that point)

---

## Quick Reference

| Expression | Use |
|------------|-----|
| **As** | same time |
| **As if** | comparison |
| **During** | within period |
| **For** | duration |
| **While** | same time action |
| **By** | deadline |
| **Until** | up to point |
| **By the time** | reference point |
