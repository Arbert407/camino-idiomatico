# Verb + Preposition: From, On, In, Into, With - Grammar Guide

## Introduction

Additional verb + preposition combinations for IT communication.

---

## Verb + From

### Source/Origin

| Verb + from | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **receive from** | get |
| **hear from** | get news |
| **differ from** | be different |
| **suffer from** | experience |

> "We received feedback **from** the users."
> "The error **differs from** the previous one."

---

## Verb + On

### Attachment/State

| Verb + on | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **depend on** | rely on |
| **insist on** | demand |
| **count on** | rely on |
| **carry on** | continue |

> "We **depend on** the server."
> "He **insists on** testing."

---

## Verb + In

### Enclosed Context

| Verb + in | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **believe in** | trust |
| **invest in** | put money |
| **succeed in** | achieve |
| **result in** | cause |

> "We **believe in** the cloud solution."
> "The update **resulted in** improvements."

---

## Verb + Into

### Movement Inside

| Verb + into | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **convert into** | transform |
| **translate into** | change language |
| **divide into** | separate |
| **talk into** | persuade |

> "We **converted the system into** a cloud platform."
> "**Translate the document into** Spanish."

---

## Verb + With

### Association

| Verb + with | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **work with** | collaborate |
| **provide with** | supply |
| **agree with** | concur |
| **deal with** | handle |

> "I'm **working with** the network team."
> "We **provide clients with** support."

---

## Uses in IT

### From/On/In
> "We **heard from** the client."
> "It **depends on** the configuration."
> "We **believe in** this approach."

### Into/With
> "**Translate the text into** JSON."
> "I'm **working with** the development team."

---

## Practice Exercises

### Complete:

1. We received feedback ___ the users. → **from**
2. The system depends ___ the config. → **on**
3. We believe ___ cloud solutions. → **in**
4. Translate the file ___ JSON. → **into**
5. Working ___ the team on this. → **with**

---

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "depend of" (wrong)
> ✅ "depend **on**"

### ❌ "provide to" (wrong)
> ✅ "provide **with**" or "provide **to**"

---

## Quick Summary

| Combination | Use |
|-------------|-----|
| verb + from | source |
| verb + on | attachment |
| verb + in | enclosed |
| verb + into | movement inside |
| verb + with | association |
